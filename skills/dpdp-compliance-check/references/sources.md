# Sources

Rules as of 2026-11-01. A bullet is either a page that was used or an explicit UNVERIFIED tag.

- [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (MeitY PDF)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf) — opened 2026-11-01. Sections 2(f), 2(t), 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, and 16. Child means a person who has not completed 18 years. Section 16 is a power to restrict transfers, not a general ban.
- [India Code, Act 22 of 2023](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/22037) — Schedule opened 2026-11-01. Maximum penalties: ₹250 crore (section 8(5)), ₹200 crore (section 8(6)), ₹200 crore (section 9), ₹150 crore (section 10), ₹10,000 (section 15, Data Principal duties), undertaking penalty as applicable (section 32), ₹50 crore (any other provision). SPEC §2 had listed the ₹10,000 and ₹50 crore rows as UNVERIFIED. The Schedule text confirms them.
- [G.S.R. 843(E), 13 November 2025 (MeitY)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) — commencement of the Act. Gazette imprint CG-DL-E-14112025-267647. Immediate, one-year, and eighteen-month groups. Eighteen months after 13 November 2025 is 13 May 2027. Section 33 is in the eighteen-month group, so the Schedule is not imposable before that date.
- [DPDP Rules, 2025 (MeitY catalogue)](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa) — ministry page for G.S.R. 846(E), notified 13 November 2025. Rule 1: rules 1, 2, and 17 to 21 on publication; rule 4 after one year (13 November 2026); rules 3, 5 to 16, 22, and 23 after eighteen months (13 May 2027).
- [English text of the DPDP Rules, 2025](https://www.dpdpa.in/dpdpa_rules_2025/dpdpa_rules_english_2.pdf) — gazette text opened 2026-11-01. Rules 3, 6, 7 (72 hours), 8 (48 hours, one-year logs, three-year Third Schedule), 9, 10, 12, 13 (12-month DPIA), 14 (90 days), 15, and the First Schedule (Consent Manager net worth of ₹2 crore).
- Third Schedule user-count line — UNVERIFIED. The English PDF text layer splits columns, so "not less than two crore" can be misread as "less than". The gaming row shows "not less than" fifty lakh. The checklist prints the counts and sets `confirm` true.
