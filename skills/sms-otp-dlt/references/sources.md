# Sources

Rules as of 2026-11-01. A bullet is either a page that was used or an explicit UNVERIFIED tag.

- [TCCCPR Second Amendment Regulations, 2025](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Regulation_12022025.pdf) — opened 2026-11-01. Header of up to 11 characters. Suffixes `-P`, `-S`, `-T`, and `-G` in regulation 17 (Schedule I). Transactional messages are within 30 minutes of a customer-initiated transaction. Promotional messages need an opt-out in the same message. Regulation 17 comes into force 60 days after gazette publication. The notification is dated 12 February 2025, so this skill uses 13 April 2025. SPEC §1.13 called the suffixes UNVERIFIED. The amendment states them.
- [Direction of 30 August 2024](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-09/Direction_30082024_0.pdf) — URLs, APKs, and OTT links that are not whitelisted are blocked from 1 October 2024. Callback numbers were deferred.
- [TRAI press release 53/2024](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-10/PR_No.53of2024.pdf) — 20 August 2024. The original note said 1 September 2024 and included callback numbers. Superseded in part by the 30 August direction.
- [TRAI press release 60/2024](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-10/PR_No.60of2024.pdf) — extends URL, APK, and OTT whitelisting to 1 October 2024 and says the callback timeline will be fixed separately.
- [TRAI press release 92/2024](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-12/PR_No.92of2024.pdf) — from 11 December 2024, SMS on an unregistered PE–TM path is rejected.
- [Direction of 30 November 2024](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-12/Direction_30112024.pdf) — reject messages from 11 December 2024 where the telemarketer chain is undefined or does not match.
- [TRAI consolidated TCCCPR text, 21 May 2026](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-05/CA_21052026.pdf) — Schedule II note says bands 00:00–10:00 and 21:00–24:00 are default OFF unless the customer switches them on. The file says the gazette prevails if they differ. Promotional hours are UNVERIFIED as an absolute ban.
- [MSG91 SendOTP](https://docs.msg91.com/otp/sendotp) — opened 2026-11-01. POST `https://control.msg91.com/api/v5/otp`. `template_id` and `mobile` are query parameters. `authkey` is a header. `otp_length` is 4 to 9, default 4. `otp_expiry` is 1 to 10080 minutes, default 15. The template contains `##OTP##`. UNVERIFIED against TRAI and MeitY. This is a vendor document.
- [MSG91 DLT content-template FAQ](https://msg91.com/help/dlt-registration-in-india/dlt-content-template-faqs) — `{#var#}`, brand name, variable not last, and a variable cap. One MSG91 page says 30 characters and another says 40. UNVERIFIED against TRAI. Warnings use `source` `operator`. The linter warns above 30 characters, matching SPEC §1.13.
