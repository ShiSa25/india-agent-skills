# Sources

Rules as of 2026-11-01. A bullet is either a page that was opened or an explicit UNVERIFIED tag.

- [RBI Indian Currency FAQ, updated 15 Apr 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/INDIANCURRENCY15042025.pdf) — opened 2026-11-01. One rupee consists of 100 paise.
- [RBI explanatory notes for bank annual accounts](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/Explanatory.aspx) — opened 2026-11-01. Unit 1 lakh = 100000. The same notes offer Rs. lakh and Rs. crore as reporting units. One crore is 100 lakh (1,00,00,000). Grouping is the last three digits, then groups of two.
- Amounts at or above one hundred crore spelled as hundred crore, thousand crore, lakh crore, or crore crore, and not arab — UNVERIFIED as a cheque-words statute. RBI names lakh and crore and does not use arab in that note. The script sets `confirm` true when this convention is used.
- Short forms `1.2 L`, `1.2 Cr`, `1.2 L Cr`, and `1.2 Cr Cr` — UNVERIFIED abbreviations. They are not taken from `Intl`. The script sets `confirm` true when a short form is used.
