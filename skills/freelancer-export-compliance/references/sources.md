- A return to nine months by FEMA 23(R)/(8)/2026-RB dated 5 Jun 2026 — UNVERIFIED. That instrument was not on the RBI notification page opened 2026-11-01.
- Invoice date as the start of the FEMA clock for non-software services before 1 Oct 2026 — UNVERIFIED. Regulation 9 of the 2015 regulations runs from the date of export. The invoice-date sentence in the consolidated text is written for software that is not in physical form.
- FIRA or FIRC as the name of the realisation advice — UNVERIFIED. The 2026 regulations require the authorised dealer to mark off EDPMS when export value is realised. They do not name FIRA or FIRC.
- e-BRC for every services exporter — UNVERIFIED. [DGFT](https://www.dgft.gov.in/) was not opened to a public notice that makes e-BRC compulsory for services. The skill treats it as optional, or required only when a scheme asks for it.
