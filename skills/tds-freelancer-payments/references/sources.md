# Sources

Rules as of 2026-11-01. A bullet is a page that was read, or it carries the tag UNVERIFIED.

- [Income-tax Act, 2025, s.393, serial 6](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-393-6) — opened 2026-11-01. Contractor 1% / 2% with ₹30,000 and ₹1,00,000; individual or HUF not on the main rows, 2% above fifty lakh rupees; professional 10% and technical, film-royalty, and call-centre 2%, with ₹50,000 and no threshold for a director. Deduction is on the entire amount once the sum or the aggregate exceeds the threshold.
- [FAQ on the higher rate under s.397(2)](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/faqs-on-higher-rate-of-tds-under-section-397) — opened 2026-11-01. If PAN is not furnished, the highest of the section rate, the rate in force, and 20%. The 5% ceiling is only for s.393(1) Table serial 8(ii) and 8(v).
- [Income-tax Act, 2025, as amended by the Finance Act, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf) — the extract that was retrieved did not include the deduction chapter, so s.393 and s.397 were taken from the two pages above, not from this PDF.
- Form 26Q section codes 1023, 1024, 1026, 1027, and 1028 — UNVERIFIED. They are not printed on the s.393 page. Return-preparation tables use them for contractor, technical, professional, and director rows. Serial 6(ii) (old s.194M) has no code in those tables.
- Old s.206AB was not carried into the 2025 Act — UNVERIFIED. The s.393 page and the s.397 FAQ do not mention a non-filer higher rate. The full bare Act text of the deduction chapter was not in the PDF extract. This skill does not apply s.206AB.
- Income-tax Act, 1961, ss.194C, 194J, 194M, and 206AA, as on 31 Mar 2026 — UNVERIFIED. The bare 1961 text was not opened. The script uses those section labels before 1 Apr 2026, with the same numbers the 2025 Act states, and sets confirm.
