# Disclaimer

This tool gives general information and format checks only, not tax, legal or financial advice. Rules are as of 2026-11-01 and change often. A valid format does not prove that an ID is registered or active. Confirm with the official source or a qualified professional before filing, invoicing or paying.

Nothing in this repository files a return, calls the GST portal or an IRP, moves money, or proves that an ID was issued. A skill whose rule data says `verified: false` must set `confirm: true` and tell you to confirm the point. The open list of those points is in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md).

Public GSTINs that appear in tests are the company and platform numbers named in SPEC §1.1, used only to check a checksum. Do not add a real individual's PAN or Aadhaar.
